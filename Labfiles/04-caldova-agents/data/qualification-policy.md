# Caldova qualification policy (POL-01)

This synthetic policy is for training, not legal or regulatory advice.

- A CMO needs Approved qualification status and an explicit ISO 8601 expiry date. Missing, malformed, expired, or launch-window-expiring evidence makes it ineligible. An expiry equal to the launch date is valid for this exercise.
- A candidate must supply the entire remaining gap in this simplified scenario. Splitting an order across CMOs is outside scope.
- Its transfer must complete by November 30, 2026, calculated from October 1, 2026. Transfer duration already includes the prepared readiness allowance. Do not assume that real technology transfers follow this simplified model.
- Manufacturing and batch processing must use the UK location for this exercise. A UK field in a sample record is not proof of an end-to-end residency guarantee.
- Plant batches must be Released. Capacity and supplier evidence must contain audit references.
- The Compliance Guardrail independently checks every proposed CMO against source records. It runs even when the remaining gap is zero. A tool error, absent evidence, or failed check blocks release of a positive recommendation. Never return an unchecked draft as a fallback.
- Conflicting capacity records require a Needs review result, with no numeric capacity conclusion and no recommended CMO. Ask the data owner to resolve the conflict.
- The workflow creates decision-support evidence. It does not approve procurement, qualify a supplier, sign an electronic record, or establish GxP or 21 CFR Part 11 compliance.
